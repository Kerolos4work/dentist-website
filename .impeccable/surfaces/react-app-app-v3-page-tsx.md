Scope: `react-app/app/v3/page.tsx` — separate single-page V3, visitor mode Persuade.

Audience/job: Cairo patients comparing a cosmetic dentist from social and review sources; they want proof, specialty clarity, location, fee, and a booking action.

Action/proof: Book on Vezeeta or call; proof is real Instagram before/after imagery plus Google and Vezeeta reviews and ratings.

Constraints: preserve V1 routes; shared Header/Footer; RTL Arabic; verified facts only from `site.ts`; no invented claims; 390px mobile must not overflow. Build path: code-first.

Direction contract

THESIS: A technical smile blueprint, not an editorial folio. The page reads like a drafted treatment plan: measurements, indices, verified readouts, and approved case plates arranged on a diagnostic grid.

OWN-WORLD: Ink ground, cyan measurement grid and reticles, red approval stamp. Cairo carries oversized Arabic display, Anton carries coordinates and specimen labels, Pinyon Script signs the doctor record. The grammar is drafting table: modular panels, hairline grids, corner ticks, numbered axes, sparse clinical annotations.

STORY: The visitor sees the premise, reads verified readouts, maps the eight specialties, inspects case plates, follows the three-step protocol, checks the doctor record and patient notes, locates the clinic, then approves the booking action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Technical header with coordinate label, oversized Arabic premise on the left, portrait in a scan frame on the right, and a compact verified-readout bar pinned below. Red appears only in the decisive action and approval stamp.

FORM: Smile Blueprint / diagnostic drafting grid.
